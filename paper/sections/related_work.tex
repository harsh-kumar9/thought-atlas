\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Behavioral and temporal descriptions.}
\citet{gandhi2025habits} study verification, backtracking, subgoals, and backward chaining; \citet{kim2026society} connect conversational behaviors to learning and intervention. We use both vocabularies as overlapping textual annotations. LOT characterizes model patterns and style interventions \citep{chen2025lot}; ReasonOps studies recurring operations across models and benchmarks \citep{lee2026reasonops}; ThinkARM analyzes functional episodes under efficiency interventions \citep{li2025thinkarm}. Our emphasis is what within-model task differences and matched demands do, and do not, imply for intervention.

\paragraph{Steering and reward learning.}
SEAL already transfers math-derived steering to planning and compares efficiency baselines \citep{chen2025seal}. \citet{sinii2025vectors} train residual steering vectors with RL. Our contribution is neither the existence of transfer nor bias-only RL: it is the multi-source comparison through one behavioral construction, exposing unequal fidelity, completion-dependent outcomes, and similar annotation shifts shared with a prior method.

\paragraph{Task organization and cognitive control.}
Shared human control networks and primate population dynamics adapt to task context \citep{cole2013hubs,mante2013context}. Resource-rational accounts relate allocation to benefits and costs \citep{shenhav2013control,lieder2020resource}; metareasoning applies this perspective to LLM computation \citep{desabbata2024metareasoning}. These connections motivate our task and budget comparisons without establishing optimal allocation or neural correspondence.

\paragraph{Limits of textual explanation.}
Reasoning text can omit influences on answers \citep{turpin2023faithfulness,chen2025faithfulness}. Accordingly, our signatures describe emitted operations: decoding task identity does not identify its mechanism, and changing annotations alongside accuracy does not establish mediation. Intervention fidelity and distinct outcome protocols constrain interpretation.
